Flappy Frog Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Frog ("Flappy Frog", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Flappy Frog mobile game and related web services.

Account deletion: If you want to delete your Flappy Frog account and associated account data, use the account-deletion request method described below.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address when you use email/password sign-in.
  • Google account information made available through Google Sign-In, such as display name, email address, and provider/account identifier.
  • A Firebase Authentication user identifier associated with your account.
  • Your Flappy Frog nickname/display name.

Game and leaderboard information

For signed-in players, Flappy Frog may store account-associated game information including best score, submitted scores, daily/weekly/monthly/all-time leaderboard scores, games played, Flies, Butterflies, milestone information, leaderboard eligibility, and relevant timestamps. Guests can play without appearing on the signed-in-player leaderboard.

Information stored on your device

Flappy Frog uses local storage for game and interface state, which can include your nickname, local best score, games played, currency balances, milestone status, and other gameplay state needed for the game.

Firebase service information

Flappy Frog uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase Authentication may automatically process information such as IP address, user-agent information, Firebase Android App ID, and a Firebase user identifier for authentication, security, and service operation. Information stored in Firestore depends on the features you use and the game data sent to it.

Advertising information

Flappy Frog uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including rewarded and interstitial ads. Depending on the device, settings, consent status, and ad-serving configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, advertising or app/device identifiers, ad interactions, app launches/video views, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2. How we use information

  • Create and maintain accounts.
  • Authenticate users and help secure accounts.
  • Save nicknames, scores, leaderboard results, and game progress.
  • Operate account and leaderboard features.
  • Provide gameplay and rewarded-ad features.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Flappy Frog.

3. Sharing of information

Flappy Frog uses service providers that help operate the game, including Google Firebase for authentication and cloud database services, Google Sign-In when selected by the user, and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the services we use to protect information. Firebase states that applicable Firebase data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Data retention

We retain account and game information for as long as needed to provide Flappy Frog, operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the service. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete associated account data that we control, subject to data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or similar reasons.
