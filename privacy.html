Flappy Frog Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Frog ("Flappy Frog", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Flappy Frog mobile game and related web services.

Account deletion: You can request deletion directly from the Flappy Frog app through Profile → Delete Account, or use the Flappy Frog Account Deletion page if you no longer have the app installed.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address when you use email/password sign-in.
  • Google account information made available through Google Sign-In, such as display name, email address, and provider/account identifier.
  • A Firebase Authentication user identifier associated with your account.
  • Your Flappy Frog nickname/display name.

Game and leaderboard information

For signed-in players, Flappy Frog may store account-associated game information including best score, submitted scores, daily/weekly/monthly/all-time leaderboard scores, games played, Flies, Butterflies, milestone information, leaderboard eligibility, and relevant timestamps. Guests can play without appearing on the signed-in-player leaderboard.

Information stored on your device

Flappy Frog uses local storage for game and interface state, which can include your nickname, local best score, games played, currency balances, milestone status, and other gameplay state needed for the game.

Firebase service information

Flappy Frog uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase Authentication may automatically process information such as IP address, user-agent information, Firebase Android App ID, and a Firebase user identifier for authentication, security, and service operation. Information stored in Firestore depends on the features you use and the game data sent to it.

Advertising information

Flappy Frog uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including rewarded and interstitial ads. Depending on the device, settings, consent status, and ad-serving configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, advertising or app/device identifiers, ad interactions, app launches/video views, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2. How we use information

  • Create and maintain accounts.
  • Authenticate users and help secure accounts.
  • Save nicknames, scores, leaderboard results, and game progress.
  • Operate account and leaderboard features.
  • Provide gameplay and rewarded-ad features.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Flappy Frog.

3. Sharing of information

Flappy Frog uses service providers that help operate the game, including Google Firebase for authentication and cloud database services, Google Sign-In when selected by the user, and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the services we use to protect information. Firebase states that applicable Firebase data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Data retention

We retain account and game information for as long as needed to provide Flappy Frog, operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the service. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete the Firebase Authentication account and the associated Flappy Frog cloud player data that we control, including the player's leaderboard/profile data, subject to data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or similar reasons. Advertising and technical information processed directly by Google services may be subject to Google's own retention policies and controls.

6. Account Deletion

Flappy Frog provides account deletion both inside the app and through an external web resource. Signed-in users can open Profile → Delete Account in the app and confirm the deletion. The app deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the associated Flappy Frog cloud player document, including leaderboard/profile data that we control.

If you no longer have the app installed, use the Flappy Frog Account Deletion page. The page lets you sign in with your Flappy Frog Google or email account and directly request permanent deletion.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Local-only game data stored on a device may remain until the app's local data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Data that a third-party service must retain under its own legal, security, fraud-prevention, or service-retention requirements may not be immediately deleted by Flappy Frog.

7. Children's privacy

Flappy Frog is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us using the developer contact information above.

8. Third-party services

Flappy Frog relies on third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including Firebase, Google Sign-In, and Google Mobile Ads. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and terms.

9. Your choices

You can choose whether to create or sign in to an account. You can control certain advertising and privacy choices through Android, Google, and any applicable in-app privacy controls. You may request deletion of your Flappy Frog account as described above.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Flappy Frog's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
